Your computer is not locked

We closed a fake “your computer has been locked” page. It was generated inside your browser by an unknown site and pretended to be a security warning so you would call a fake support number.

Don’t call any number the page showed, and don’t give anyone remote access to your computer.

Why it was blocked

    Details

    Scam pages often tell you to ignore warnings like this one. Only continue if you are sure this is a page you trust, such as an internal tool you use every day, and nobody told you to do this.

    If you continue, this site is allowed until you close the browser. It still won’t be able to lock your keyboard, stop you from leaving, or trap you in pop-up loops.

    Detection runs entirely in your browser. Nothing was sent anywhere.
